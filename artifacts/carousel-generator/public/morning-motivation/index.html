Morning Motivation

A little 5 to 10 second reel to start someone's day. Pick a scene, pick a message, add your logo, download.

1. Pick your scene

Click a scene to see it in the phone. Tick the box on every scene you want in your batch, then use step 7 to make them all at once.

Toast look

Upload your plain toast clip, the one where the toast pops up with nothing written on it. About 5 seconds works best. Your words burn onto the toast after it settles.

Use your own photo of plain toast, or let me make one. Until a photo is added you will see the illustrated toast.

The video is made by Veo, which bills your Google key for each clip, then your words are added on top. You can also upload your own video of this scene.

2. Choose your message

Or write your own below (keep it short and punchy, 90 characters is the sweet spot).

3. Make it yours

Download transparent PNG

Making a sample for someone who is not a client? Upload their logo here and I take the background away for you. For clients, their own logo loads when you choose them in step 4.

5. Schedule

The reel is recorded, uploaded and booked for you. Stories do not carry a caption, so none is sent.

6. Download

The video is silent, so you can add trending audio in Instagram. It records in real time, so it takes as long as the reel lasts.

7. Bulk: make several at once

Line up a few reels, then write all the captions, schedule them all or download them all in one go. New reels use the scene, client and post type you have picked above, and you can change any of them in the list.

Quick start: tick the scenes you want in step 1, choose the wording here, then press the first button. You get one reel per ticked scene.

Scheduling uses the post type and reply word from the steps above. Reels are recorded one after another in real time, so ten reels takes a few minutes. Keep this tab open while it works. Chrome may ask once to allow multiple downloads.
